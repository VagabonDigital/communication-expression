Cultural Lens

Communication rules across cultures

Communication is not just about exchanging information — it also reflects deep ideas about respect, hierarchy, and social harmony. Across cultures and history, people have developed very different rules about when to speak clearly, when to stay vague, and when silence or emotion carries more weight than words.
